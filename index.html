What is Clear Food?+

Clear Food is a food-scanning app. Take a photo to explore a personalized food score, insights for your goals, calories, macros and food alternatives.

Can I use Clear Food on Android?+

Not just yet. Join the Android waitlist to hear when it's ready. People who join before launch will get one month free in the app when the Android version is released.

What happens when I get started here?+

You'll try a short preview of our onboarding: choose your goals and preferences, then see your personal starting point. No account is needed. This preview doesn't create an app profile or sync your answers to the app.

Is Clear Food medical advice?+

No. Clear Food provides food information to support everyday choices. Scores are estimates, not a diagnosis or a guarantee of health outcomes. For medical concerns, allergies or a prescribed diet, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
