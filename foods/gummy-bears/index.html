Do gummy bears cause acne?

A 100 g bag of gummy bears scores 13. It is almost pure sugar, 59 g per bag, with no fibre, fat or protein to slow it down. High-sugar diets are linked to more acne. A bowl of fresh berries scores 90 and still tastes sweet.
