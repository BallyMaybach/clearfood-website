Does whey protein cause acne?

A whey shake scores 85 in Clear Food: 24 g of protein, almost no sugar and 95 for muscle. For skin it is less clear. Whey is made from milk, and milk is linked to acne in studies of young people. For whey itself there are only small studies and case reports, no large trials. If you break out after starting whey, pausing it for a few weeks is an easy test.
